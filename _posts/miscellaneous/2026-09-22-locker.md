I believe my website should not only be a place for boring technical articles but also a place to share my life experiences.

It was just a normal day when I found myself in a situation that required me to break a padlock.

I went to a gym to work out. Normally, I did not wear a jacket because it was summer. However, it is September now, and the weather is getting colder. 

I decided to wear a jacket to the gym. When I arrived, I went to the locker room to store my belongings. I found a locker that was available and placed my bag and my jacket inside. I then locked the locker with a padlock. However, I immediately realized that I had forgotten the key in my jacket pocket, which I had left in my locker. I was locked out of my own locker, and I had no way to retrieve my belongings.

At first, I thought about going back to my apartment to get the key, but I realized that my apartment key was also in my jacket pocket, which was locked in the locker.

Normally, after I clear my locker after I finish my workout, I would take the padlock off and lock it so that the next time, I must check if I have the key before I lock my locker. However, the last time I had gone to gym, I forgot to lock the padlock after I finished my workout, and this time, I forgot to check if I had the key before I locked my locker.

I contacted the gym staff to see if they could help me unlock the padlock. They gave me a bolt cutter. I was not sure how to use it and ask her to help break my padlock. She said she could not enter the man's locker room, so she could not help me. I was left with no choice but to figure out how to break the padlock myself.

I could not do it for several minutes. Several people in the locker room were looking at me and eventually helped me. But we still cannot break the padlock. Finally, there was a man who was also staff at the gym. He was able to break the padlock with that bolt cutter. I was very grateful for his help. 

This experience is not uncommon. Many people have experienced being locked out of their own lockers, such as [this Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/PlanetFitnessMembers/comments/1sc9wpr/has_anyone_accidentally_locked_their_key_in_the/).

These are some tips to avoid being locked out of your own locker:
- Use a combination lock instead of a padlock, so you don't have to worry about losing a key!
- After you finish your workout, lock the padlock so that the next time you use the locker, you must check if you have the key before you lock it.
- There is a person suggesting a method called ["pointing and calling"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointing_and_calling).

Finally, if you need to break a padlock, you should position the bolt cutter to be perpendicular to the shackle of the padlock. This will allow you to apply maximum force and break the padlock more easily. Moreover, the shackle should be put close to the pivot point of the bolt cutter. This will allow you to apply more force to the shackle and break it more easily.

I create [a page](/boltcutter/) to show how to use a bolt cutter to break a padlock, why perpendicular positioning is important, and how to position the shackle close to the pivot point of the bolt cutter.

So, I hope this article can help you avoid being locked out of your own locker and provide some tips on how to break a padlock if you ever find yourself in that situation. However, if possible, ask gym staff for help first since you may break a locker if you do not know how to use a bolt cutter properly.
